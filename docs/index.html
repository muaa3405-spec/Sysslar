Sysslar

Your tasks. Your time. Your computer. Nobody else’s business.

Sysslar is a small desktop app for Windows and macOS that keeps track of everything you want to get done, and shows where your time actually goes. It sits in the system tray or the macOS menu bar and stores everything in a database on your own computer.

No account. No subscription. No telemetry. It works on a plane, behind a firewall, anywhere.

What it does

  • Tasks with groups, priorities, due dates and reminders
  • Notes, with attachments on both tasks and notes
  • Time tracking per task, with reports
  • Several workspaces, kept separate
  • Automatic daily backups, kept for 14 days
  • Export to CSV and Markdown, and import from CSV

Syncing between your computers

Sync is optional. When you turn it on, Sysslar encrypts everything with AES-256 before it leaves your computer, using a key that exists only on the computers you turn sync on. The encrypted files are then stored in cloud storage you already have — Google Drive, iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or any shared folder.

Nobody but you can read the synced data — not the developer, and not the storage provider, because the key never leaves your computers.
